## Why two formats

The cohort modules and the crash course cover the same ground. The split is about pacing:

- **Modules** have natural breakpoints between sessions and explicit Defend-It questions at each stage. Better when an instructor is pacing the class.
- **Crash course** flows top-to-bottom without breakpoints. Better when you're working alone at your own speed.

Both produce the same outcome: a working public site, four transferable craft skills, and a reusable template you can fork for your own subject.
