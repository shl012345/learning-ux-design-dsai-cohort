The four lessons in this section are the ones that should arguably come on day one of every cohort, and almost never do. They're about *how to learn*, not about any specific tool or library. If you're a few weeks into your cohort and the volume of new ideas feels like drinking from a firehose, this section is for you.

Read these before — or alongside — the working-with-ai section. They set up the mental moves you'll lean on for everything else.
