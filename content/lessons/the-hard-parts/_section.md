The three lessons here are about the *judgement calls* that separate a working demo from something that survives contact with real users. None of these are taught in a notebook; all of them get learned on the job, usually painfully.

Read this section once your hands-on builds are starting to feel real — when you've got something deployed and you're staring at it wondering whether to ship.
