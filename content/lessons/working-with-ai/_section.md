Every cohort student is using AI tools. Most are using them in a "prompt → accept → paste" loop that quietly costs them the learning. The three lessons in this section are about the *discipline* of working with AI — prompting that earns its keep, knowing when to put the AI down, and verifying outputs before you act on them.

Comes after the foundations section because the verification habits in particular lean on the "knowing vs understanding" lesson.
